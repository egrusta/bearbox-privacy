BEARBOX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

In one sentence: the BEARBOX Android app collects nothing, sends nothing, and keeps everything it remembers on your own device.

1. Who we are.

BEARBOX is an independent game developed and published by Egis Bark, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: support.bearbox@gmail.com. This policy is published at: [PRIVACY POLICY URL — TBD].

2. What this policy covers.

This policy covers the BEARBOX app for Android downloaded from Google Play. The browser version of BEARBOX, played at perchance.org, is a different service running on Perchance's platform and is covered by Perchance's own privacy policy — this policy does not describe it.

3. What the app does not do.

The app has no account and no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no real-money in-app purchases, and no server of any kind. It does not connect to any server, and it does not transmit any information about you or your device off your device.

4. Data we collect: none.

We do not collect, receive, store on a server, sell or share any personal data — and no other data about you or your play either. There is no account, no identifier and no telemetry.

5. What is stored on your device (and never uploaded).

The app keeps its progress in its own private local storage on your device:

  • coins, unlocked catalogue items, purchased cosmetics and settings;
  • play statistics, missions and discoveries progress, and adventure progress;
  • the contraptions you saved in "MY BUILDS";
  • the list of BEAT MY BUILD builds that have already paid their coins;
  • an optional display name, if you type one in.

None of this leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we have no way to read it.

6. Permissions.

The app declares only the INTERNET permission, which is included automatically by the WebView packaging tool the app is built with. The app does not use it: it makes no network requests. The app does not request or use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone, SMS, photos or file storage.

7. Sharing a contraption.

The app can export a build so you can pass it to someone. That exported data is just your build — never your progress, coins or identity — and the app hands it to your device's own share or save feature; it goes wherever you choose to send it. The app itself uploads nothing.

8. Children.

The app is a cartoon physics sandbox suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

9. Third parties.

None. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Its only third-party component is the Rapier physics engine, a local library bundled inside the app (Apache-2.0 licence).

10. Deleting your data.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. To erase everything the app has stored on your device, use Settings → RESET SAVE inside the game, or uninstall the app — uninstalling removes all of it. Because we hold no personal data, data-protection rights such as access, correction, erasure or portability have nothing to apply to.

11. Changes to this policy.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the address above with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Questions.

Email support.bearbox@gmail.com.
